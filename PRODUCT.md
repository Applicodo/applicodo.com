# Applicodo

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain HTML, CSS and minimal JavaScript. Cloudflare Pages serves public/; a Pages Function verifies Turnstile and sends contact enquiries through Resend.

## Users

Founders and businesses seeking direct collaboration with an independent iOS developer.

## Product Purpose

Explain Applicodo's native iOS development service, demonstrate real work through CaptionFast, and invite a project enquiry.

## Positioning

An independent developer: direct communication and hands-on iOS development, rather than agency scale or an invented team.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- English, responsive, accessible marketing page.
- Only name, email, project message and anti-bot token enter the contact handler. No accounts, payments, uploads, lead database, auto-replies or analytics.
- Enquiries go only to contact@applicodo.com through a dedicated verified Resend sender.
- Keep Cloudflare Pages project applicodo and existing Google Workspace mail routing. No paid upgrades.
- Remove physical address, maps, obsolete contact details and legacy commerce pages from deployment.
- Preserve unrelated CaptionFast work.
- Keep the public note brief: "No analytics. No advertising cookies." User explicitly removed all detailed processor/legal disclosure and the expandable privacy section; provider/data-handling detail belongs in internal security documentation. Do not claim infrastructure never uses any functional/security cookie.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the Applicodo name and refresh the identity. Bold typography, generous spacing, graphite text, light backgrounds and confident blue. The user chose visual-concept-first and must review a comp before website UI is coded.

## Evidence on Hand

- CaptionFast is an iPhone captioning app sold by APPLICODO S.R.L.: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/captionfast-auto-ai-captions/id6757338308
- Verified capabilities: transcription, caption styles, word highlighting, positioning/timing controls and video export.
- Existing neutral images: CaptionFast website export-in-4k.png and languages.png; app logo. Keep origins with shipping copies.
- No approved commercial statistics, testimonials, ratings, customer list, or performance claims.

## Product Principles

- Real product work earns trust.
- Direct, concrete language.
- The contact form is the sole contact channel on the page.
- Lightweight delivery and a small attack surface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable navigation/form, visible focus, WCAG AA contrast, descriptive labels, accessible statuses, and reduced-motion support.
