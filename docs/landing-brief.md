# Landing surface brief

## Confirmed intent

Persuade founders and businesses to enquire about independent native iOS development. The sole conversion is a project message; CaptionFast is the proof. Refresh identity; no physical address or obsolete contact channels. User chose visual concept first.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A developer's work should carry the introduction. Clear personal service and real app screens replace stock-office imagery.

OWN-WORLD: Light canvas, graphite sans-serif, confident blue planes, open compositions, unboxed product proof and restrained ruled rows. The blue contact close anchors the page.

STORY: Understand independent iOS development, see CaptionFast as real shipped work, understand the collaboration, and send a project enquiry.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Applicodo wordmark and Work / Approach / Let's talk navigation; a strong iOS service statement and primary enquiry action; real CaptionFast app screens carry product proof. The three comps test the relative scale and sequence before code.

FORM: User-pinned direction; no random roll or seed key applies. The user explicitly approved: "bold typography, generous spacing, graphite text, light backgrounds, and a confident blue accent," then selected A — Open canvas. The approved comp itself is the quality-bar reference; no catalog card was selected. Approved comp: .impeccable/mocks/concept-a.png. User selected A — Open canvas on 2026-10-25. Product imagery is sourced; generated comp text and imagery must be translated to semantic HTML and original app assets, not shipped as a screenshot.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Delivery and storage budget

Total owned dependencies, generated mocks, build and review captures must stay below 512 MiB. Retain only chosen comp, final design documentation and one capture per reviewed viewport after approval/review. No builds or mutations in CaptionFast.
